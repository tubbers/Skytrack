Declination (12.7° for Sydney) converts magnetic north to true north. Compass offset is the extra correction for your phone's own error — Align on a star sets it for you, or drag it by hand.

Speed-adaptive: it damps hard while the phone is nearly still, then opens up as you swing so it doesn't feel laggy. Measured on 2° of input jitter: 60 leaves 0.8° with 20 ms of lag, 100 leaves 0.6° with 30 ms, 0 is effectively off.
